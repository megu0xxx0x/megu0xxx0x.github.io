Capybara Survivor (カピバラサバイバー)

Support

Help with controls, play records, ads and purchases, online battles and privacy.

日本語

Basic controls

Hold your device sideways. Use the stick on the left side of the screen to move, and the stick on the right side to aim and shoot. Press and hold an upgrade card to choose it. You can take a break with the pause button on the screen.

In the PC edition, move with the WASD keys or the arrow keys, and click to aim and shoot. Press P to pause.

If the game freezes

Close the App and open it again. In the PC edition, quit the game and start it again. If a save from the middle of a run remains, you can continue from the saved point. It may not always be possible to go back to just before the freeze.

If it keeps happening, please note your device model, OS version, App version and the scene where it happened. If possible, a screenshot that shows no personal information also helps us investigate.

If you want to keep your records, please do not delete the App before contacting us. Game records are stored on your device (on your PC for the PC edition).

Ads and “Remove Ads”

In the free mobile editions (iOS and Android), ads may be shown after a run ends (for example, after game over). Depending on ad preparation and availability, ads may not be shown.

The in-app purchase “Remove Ads” offers two ways to turn off ads. The game content is the same with either.

  • Ad-Free (One-Time Purchase): pay once and ads stop for good. It does not renew. The price in Japan is ¥990. If you bought it earlier at ¥770, it keeps working as before.
  • Ad-Free (Monthly): an auto-renewable subscription with a one-month period, available in the iOS edition version 2.1 and later. The price in Japan is ¥290 per month. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, it renews automatically at the same price and is charged to your Apple Account. You can cancel at any time from “Manage Subscription” on the purchase screen, or in Settings on your iPhone or iPad > your name > Subscriptions. After you cancel, ads stay off until the end of the period.

Outside Japan, the price shown in the App Store for your region applies. If you own Ad-Free (One-Time Purchase), the monthly plan is not offered. If you have the monthly plan and buy the one-time purchase, the monthly plan does not stop automatically, so please cancel it from “Manage Subscription”.

To buy, use “Remove Ads” on the title screen or the results screen. To restore a purchase, use “Restore Purchases” on the title screen. On iOS, use the same Apple Account (App Store) you used for the purchase; on Android, use the same Google Account (Google Play). Please check the actual amount charged on the store’s purchase confirmation screen.

Restoring purchases does not move unlocked characters or play records to another device.

The PC (Steam) edition is a paid full version bought once. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Online battles (iPhone edition)

In the iPhone edition, “Online Battle (Beta)” on the title screen lets you play with other players, up to 10 people in all (up to 6 in version 2.2 and later). “Online Battle” finds other players and starts; seats that are not filled are taken by AI Keepers (computer players). “With Friends” lets you join the same room with the 6-digit passcode shown to the person who created the room. In version 2.2 and later, you can also play on iPad. The entry to battles is not shown when the iOS app runs on a Mac, or in the Android or PC (Steam) editions.

If you cannot connect, please check your connection and try again. Online battles use Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, Inc. Communication either connects directly to the other players’ devices or goes through Epic’s relay.

“Delete Online Data” on the battle screen deletes the anonymous ID and the name tag that this device uses for online battles. Only a number that does not identify you personally remains on Epic’s servers, and it is handled under Epic’s own rules. For details, see “6-2. Online play (iPhone edition)” in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy and ad settings

The App has no account of its own. Game records are stored on your device (on your PC for the PC edition) and are not sent to the Operator. The mobile editions use Google AdMob for ads (in the iOS edition version 2.0 and later, ads from other companies are also shown through AdMob mediation). Ads are shown as non-personalized ads by default; personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking in the iOS edition version 2.0 and later. In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent screen is shown on the title screen, and you can change your choices from “Ad Settings” on the title screen.

For the iPhone tracking setting, resetting or deleting the Android advertising ID, and requests to delete support information, see “Withdrawing consent and changing settings” and “Retention and deletion requests” in the Privacy Policy. Information for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland is in “11-2. For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland”.
