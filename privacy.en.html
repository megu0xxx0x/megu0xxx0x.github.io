3. Information stored on your device or PC

The App stores game progress, unlocked companions and hats, the selected character, settings and data for resuming a run on your device (on your PC for the PC edition). It also stores recent operation records for troubleshooting and the “Remove Ads” purchase status confirmed with the store. If you use online play in the iPhone edition, the App also stores your name tag choice and the device credential of Epic Online Services on your device.

This information is not sent to the Operator, and the Operator cannot view or delete it. Records may be lost if you delete the App or change devices. If you use an OS backup feature such as iCloud or Google backup, these records may be included in the backup, which is handled under that service’s own rules.

4. Advertising (mobile editions)

The mobile editions use Google AdMob. When an ad is ready, it may be shown after a run ends (for example, after game over). Preparation of the ad service may start when the App launches. Advertising information is collected automatically by Google’s ad SDK built into the App when the SDK starts and when ads are prepared or shown, and is sent to Google. The PC edition and the browser version have no ads.

The iOS edition version 2.0 and later uses AdMob mediation (a system that chooses ads from several ad companies) and also includes the ad SDKs of AppLovin Corporation (AppLovin), Unity Technologies (Unity Ads), Liftoff Mobile, Inc. (Liftoff Monetize) and Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta Audience Network). When ads are prepared or shown, these SDKs also automatically collect advertising information and send it to their companies.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. By default, ads are requested as non-personalized ads, which are not based on your past behavior. Only in the iOS edition version 2.0 and later, and only if you allow tracking (see “iOS” below), are personalized ads requested. The Android edition always requests non-personalized ads. Even with non-personalized ads, information is used for serving ads, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and maintaining and improving the service. According to Google, when non-personalized ad requests are sent to bidders, user identifiers such as advertising IDs are removed and only part of the IP address is sent.

iOS: In version 2.0.1 and later, when you open the App and the title screen appears, the App shows a short explanation and then the iOS tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency). It is shown only while you have not yet answered it (iOS shows the prompt itself only once). It is also shown to people who have removed ads with “Remove Ads”, but whichever they choose, the App does not show them ads. Only if you allow tracking does the App request personalized ads using the advertising identifier (IDFA); in regions where Google’s consent screen is shown, your choices on that screen also apply (see below). If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not used, and the game plays the same. You can change your choice at any time (see 9. Withdrawing consent and changing settings). Version 1.1 and earlier do not ask for tracking permission and do not use the IDFA. The ad SDK may handle device identifiers scoped to the app or developer. The App has turned off Google’s publisher first-party ID.

Android: The ad SDK handles the Android advertising ID and the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (see 9. Withdrawing consent and changing settings).

The countries and regions where the mobile editions are available are shown in each store, and may include countries and regions outside Japan. In the iOS edition version 2.0 and later and in the Android edition, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK at launch to check whether an ad consent message is required in your region. No consent screen is shown in Japan. In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent screen is shown (on the title screen in the iOS edition, and at launch in the Android edition), and ads are requested after you make your choices there. You can review and change your choices at any time from “Ad Settings” (広告の設定) on the title screen (see 11-2. For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). iOS versions 1.1 and earlier include the UMP SDK but do not call it.

Once a “Remove Ads” purchase is confirmed, the App shows no ads. On launches after the purchase is confirmed, the App does not initialize the ad SDK or request ads. This does not undo processing that took place before the purchase or connections that had already started before the purchase was confirmed. The App keeps communicating with the store to check purchase status.

5. In-app purchase and restore (mobile editions)

“Remove Ads” (広告を消す) on the title screen or the results screen offers two ways to stop ads from being shown: “Ad-Free (One-Time Purchase)” (広告なし（買い切り）), a one-time purchase that does not renew, and, in the iOS edition version 2.1 and later, “Ad-Free (Monthly)” (広告なし（月額）), an auto-renewable subscription with a one-month period. In this policy, “Remove Ads” refers to either of them. The monthly subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. After you cancel, ads stay off until the end of the period and are shown again after it ends. The iOS edition uses Apple’s App Store for purchases, the subscription and restores, and the Android edition uses Google Play’s billing system for purchases and restores.

The App checks the product identifier and purchase status on your device and applies the ad-free entitlement. It also asks the store for your purchase status when it launches, and shows no ads if you have purchased. For the monthly subscription, the App keeps the expiry date of the current period on your device and asks the App Store again when the period ends. The App is designed so that the Operator does not receive card numbers or payment passwords.

After changing devices or reinstalling, you can restore your purchase with “Restore Purchases” (購入を復元) on the title screen, using the same Apple Account or Google Account used for the purchase. Restoring a purchase does not move unlocked characters or play records to another device.

For purchases on Google Play, Google may provide order information to the Operator as described by Google. The Operator uses it only as needed to confirm purchases, handle refunds and inquiries, and for accounting and tax purposes.

For how payment information is handled, see:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

6-2. Online play (iPhone edition)

In the iPhone edition, “Online Battle (Beta)” (オンライン対戦（β版）) on the title screen lets you play with up to 10 people (up to 6 in version 2.2 and later). “Duel!” (決闘！) finds other players and starts; seats that are not filled are taken by AI Keepers (computer players). “With Friends” (友だちと) lets you join a room with a 6-digit passcode.

Online play uses Epic Online Services (“EOS”), provided by Epic Games, Inc. When you first look for an opponent (from version 2.3, also when you first open the leaderboard screen), the EOS device feature (Device ID) creates an anonymous credential for this device and a user ID for this game. No Epic account is needed. The App does not send your name or email address to EOS.

During a match, your name tag, chosen character, controls and match state are exchanged with the devices of other players in the same room. In online play, to make communication faster, your device tries to connect directly to the devices of other players in the same room, and in doing so your IP address may be passed to their devices. When a direct connection cannot be made, communication goes through Epic’s relay. The Operator does not collect these IP addresses.

If someone who does not know the passcode joins a room, the person who created the room can remove them.

From version 2.3, for the leaderboard, your monthly versus points, the number of counted matches and your name tag (the number of a combination of preset words) are stored on EOS servers together with the user ID for this game. Your points and name are shown on other players’ leaderboard screens in the App. Points are given in matches with other players (AI Keepers do not count). The leaderboard changes every month. If you choose “Hide me from the ranking” (ランキングに出さない), the App does not show your row on its leaderboard screen (the record stays on EOS servers). The Operator does not store match results or records on its own servers. For how EOS handles information, please see the privacy policy of Epic Games, Inc.

The battle feature is in the iPhone edition of the App, and can also be played on iPad in version 2.2 and later. In this policy, “iPhone edition” in connection with online play includes iPad in version 2.2 and later. The entry to battles is not shown when the iOS app runs on a Mac, or in the Android, PC (Steam) or web editions.

6-3. Browser version (web)

The browser version is a free edition that you can play in a web browser without installing anything (https://capybara-survivor.megu0xxx0x.workers.dev/). It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no online play, and it does not include any feature that sends your game records or actions to the Operator or to any other third party.

Game records and settings (best records, unlock progress, the character you chose, sound and smoothness settings, and so on) are stored in your browser’s storage (localStorage). Copies of the files needed to play (images, sounds and program files) are also stored in your browser’s storage (the Service Worker cache) so that you can keep playing when you are offline. Neither is sent anywhere. If you delete your browsing history or site data, your records are deleted as well. Safari (iPhone, iPad and Mac) may delete data for sites that have not been used for a while, which also deletes your records. Opening the game from your Home Screen makes this automatic deletion less likely.

The pages and files are delivered through the network of Cloudflare, Inc. As part of delivering them, your IP address, browser type, the files requested and the date and time reach Cloudflare. The Operator does not use this information for analytics or advertising. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

If you press a notice such as “App version (App Store)”, the App Store page opens in a new tab. Information on that page is handled under Apple Inc.’s privacy policy. Whether you closed a notice is stored only in your browser.

9. Withdrawing consent and changing settings

You can change or stop the handling of ad-related information as follows. Menu names and locations vary by device and OS version.

  • iOS: Even after allowing tracking in version 2.0 and later, you can turn tracking off for the App at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. When it is off, the App cannot access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request personalized ads. If you turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, apps cannot even ask for permission (Apple’s explanation).
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads, or a similar menu. After deletion, apps receive a string of zeros instead of the advertising ID (Google’s explanation). The app set ID cannot be reset directly by users; it is reset when all apps from the same developer are uninstalled, when no app has used it for 13 months, or when the device is factory reset (Android’s explanation).
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, etc.): In regions where the consent screen is shown, you can review and change your choices at any time from “Ad Settings” (広告の設定) on the title screen.
  • Online play (iPhone edition): if you do not look for opponents with “Duel!” (決闘！) or “With Friends” (友だちと) and do not open the leaderboard screen, the App does not communicate with EOS. “Delete Online Data” (オンラインのデータを消す) on the battle screen first sets your leaderboard name to nameless and hides your row from the leaderboard screen, then deletes this device’s anonymous credential. A number that does not identify you personally, with its versus points and match count, remains on EOS servers (see 10. Retention and deletion requests).
  • To stop all transmissions from the App: uninstall the App. This also deletes the game records on your device.
  • Support emails: you can ask the Operator to stop using or to delete the information it holds (see 10. Retention and deletion requests).

The iOS tracking permission and the choices on Google’s consent screen are separate settings. The App requests personalized ads only if you have allowed tracking and, in regions where the consent screen is shown, after you have made your choices on it. Your choices on the consent screen are saved on your device so that the ad SDKs in the App can refer to them.

10. Retention and deletion requests

Support information is kept only as long as needed for support and related checks, and information no longer needed is deleted. If the law requires retention, it is kept to that extent. Please do not include unnecessary personal information in emails.

To request access to, correction of, suspension of use of, or deletion of support information held by the Operator, email megu0xxx0x@gmail.com from the address you used for your inquiry, with a subject such as “Deletion request”. After confirming that the request comes from you, the Operator will respond without undue delay in accordance with applicable law and tell you the result.

  • Game records on your device or PC: the Operator cannot view or delete them. On mobile, deleting the App deletes them. On PC, after uninstalling, delete the folder listed in section 6. In the browser version, they are deleted when you delete this game’s site data in your browser (see 6-3. Browser version). Records included in OS backups can be deleted in that service’s settings.
  • Information Google handles for ads: the Operator has no means to look up or delete it for an individual user. Please use the controls described in Google’s explanation or contact Google.
  • Online play information (iPhone edition): “Delete Online Data” (オンラインのデータを消す) on the battle screen first sets your leaderboard name to nameless and hides your row from the leaderboard screen, then signs this device out of online play and deletes the EOS anonymous credential and the name tag stored on the device. According to Epic, deleting the App also deletes the device credential; however, deleting the App alone leaves your leaderboard name and row in place. To stop showing your name and row, press “Delete Online Data” before deleting the App. A new ID is created the next time you look for an opponent. This ID is not linked to your name, email address or contact details. Only a number that does not identify you personally, with its leaderboard points and match count, remains on Epic’s servers, and it is handled under Epic’s own rules. If you have a request about this record, please contact us and we will respond.
  • Purchase history: managed by Apple, Google and Valve respectively. Please use their support or account tools.
