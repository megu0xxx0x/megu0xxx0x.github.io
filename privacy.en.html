3. Information stored on your device or PC

The App stores game progress, unlocked companions and hats, the selected character, settings and data for resuming a run on your device (on your PC for the PC edition). It also stores recent operation records for troubleshooting and the “Remove Ads” purchase status confirmed with the store. If you use online play in the iPhone edition, the App also stores your name tag choice and the device credential of Epic Online Services on your device.

This information is not sent to the Operator, and the Operator cannot view or delete it. Records may be lost if you delete the App or change devices. If you use an OS backup feature such as iCloud or Google backup, these records may be included in the backup, which is handled under that service’s own rules.

4. Advertising (mobile editions)

The mobile editions use Google AdMob. When an ad is ready, it may be shown after a run ends (for example, after game over). Preparation of the ad service may start when the App launches. Advertising information is collected automatically by Google’s ad SDK built into the App when the SDK starts and when ads are prepared or shown, and is sent to Google. The PC edition has no ads.

Ads are requested as non-personalized ads, which are not based on your past behavior, and are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Even so, information is used for serving ads, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and maintaining and improving the service. According to Google, when non-personalized ad requests are sent to bidders, user identifiers such as advertising IDs are removed and only part of the IP address is sent.

iOS: The App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). As a result, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used for the App’s ads. The ad SDK may handle device identifiers scoped to the app or developer. The App has turned off Google’s publisher first-party ID.

Android: The ad SDK handles the Android advertising ID and the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings (see 9. Withdrawing consent and changing settings).

The mobile editions are currently distributed only in Japan and do not show an ad consent screen. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK is included with the ad SDK but is not currently called. If a consent screen is introduced, for example to distribute in more regions, we will update this policy beforehand and add an in-app entry point to review and change your choices.

Once a “Remove Ads” purchase is confirmed, the App shows no ads. On launches after the purchase is confirmed, the App does not initialize the ad SDK or request ads. This does not undo processing that took place before the purchase or connections that had already started before the purchase was confirmed. The App keeps communicating with the store to check purchase status.

5. In-app purchase and restore (mobile editions)

“Remove Ads” is a one-time purchase that stops ads from being shown. It is not a subscription. The iOS edition uses Apple’s App Store, and the Android edition uses Google Play’s billing system, for purchases and restores.

The App checks the product identifier and purchase status on your device and applies the ad-free entitlement. It also asks the store for your purchase status when it launches, and shows no ads if you have purchased. The App is designed so that the Operator does not receive card numbers or payment passwords.

After changing devices or reinstalling, you can restore your purchase with “購入を復元” (Restore Purchase) on the title screen, using the same Apple Account or Google Account used for the purchase. Restoring a purchase does not move unlocked characters or play records to another device.

For purchases on Google Play, Google may provide order information to the Operator as described by Google. The Operator uses it only as needed to confirm purchases, handle refunds and inquiries, and for accounting and tax purposes.

For how payment information is handled, see:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

6. PC (Steam) edition

The PC edition is a paid, one-time purchase sold on Steam. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and the App has no function that sends information to the Operator or any other third party. Game records are stored on your PC (Windows: %APPDATA%\jp.hosoda.capybarasurvivor; macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/jp.hosoda.capybarasurvivor). If you select “プライバシーポリシー” (Privacy Policy) or “サポート・お問い合わせ” (Support) on the title screen, this website opens in your default browser, not in the game window (see 12. This website).

Purchases, library management and downloads on Steam are handled by Steam, provided by Valve Corporation, under Valve’s Privacy Policy. The App does not currently save records to Steam Cloud.

6-2. Online play (iPhone edition)

In the iPhone edition, “対戦” (Battle) lets you play with up to 10 people. “オンライン対戦” (Online battle) finds other players and starts; seats that are not filled are taken by garden keepers (computer players). “友だちと” (With friends) lets you join a room with a 6-digit passcode.

Online play uses Epic Online Services (“EOS”), provided by Epic Games, Inc. When you first look for an opponent, the EOS device feature (Device ID) creates an anonymous credential for this device and a user ID for this game. No Epic account is needed. The App does not send your name or email address to EOS.

During a match, your name tag, chosen character, controls and match state are exchanged with the devices of other players in the same room. For both “Online battle” and “With friends”, communication is set to go only through Epic’s relay, so your IP address is not passed directly to other players’ devices.

If someone who does not know the passcode joins a room, the person who created the room can remove them.

The Operator does not store match results or records on its servers. For how EOS handles information, please see the privacy policy of Epic Games, Inc.

The battle feature is in the iPhone edition of the App. The entry to battles is not shown on iPad or in the Android, PC (Steam) or web editions.

8. How we protect information

The only personal information the Operator holds about users is what is contained in support emails. It is kept in the Operator’s email account and deleted when no longer needed.

The anonymous IDs and room information for online play in the iPhone edition are managed within Epic Online Services and are not stored on the Operator’s servers. The Operator may look up these IDs in Epic’s developer console as needed, for example to investigate misuse or to delete records.

Game records are stored on your device or PC and are not sent to or stored on the Operator’s servers. According to Google, all data collected by Google’s ad SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Payments are processed by each store’s own systems.

9. Withdrawing consent and changing settings

You can change or stop the handling of ad-related information as follows. Menu names and locations vary by device and OS version.

  • iOS: The App does not ask for tracking permission. If you turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, apps cannot even ask for permission and cannot access the advertising identifier (IDFA) (Apple’s explanation).
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads, or a similar menu. After deletion, apps receive a string of zeros instead of the advertising ID (Google’s explanation). The app set ID cannot be reset directly by users; it is reset when all apps from the same developer are uninstalled, when no app has used it for 13 months, or when the device is factory reset (Android’s explanation).
  • Online play (iPhone edition): if you do not look for opponents with “オンライン対戦” (Online battle) or “友だちと” (With friends), the App does not communicate with EOS. You can delete this device’s anonymous credential with “オンラインのデータを消す” (Delete online data) on the battle screen.
  • To stop all transmissions from the App: uninstall the App. This also deletes the game records on your device.
  • Support emails: you can ask the Operator to stop using or to delete the information it holds (see 10. Retention and deletion requests).

Because the App does not currently obtain ad consent in the app, there is no in-app consent settings screen.

10. Retention and deletion requests

Support information is kept only as long as needed for support and related checks, and information no longer needed is deleted. If the law requires retention, it is kept to that extent. Please do not include unnecessary personal information in emails.

To request access to, correction of, suspension of use of, or deletion of support information held by the Operator, email megu0xxx0x@gmail.com from the address you used for your inquiry, with a subject such as “Deletion request”. After confirming that the request comes from you, the Operator will respond without undue delay in accordance with applicable law and tell you the result.

  • Game records on your device or PC: the Operator cannot view or delete them. On mobile, deleting the App deletes them. On PC, after uninstalling, delete the folder listed in section 6. Records included in OS backups can be deleted in that service’s settings.
  • Information Google handles for ads: the Operator has no means to look up or delete it for an individual user. Please use the controls described in Google’s explanation or contact Google.
  • Online play information (iPhone edition): “オンラインのデータを消す” (Delete online data) on the battle screen signs this device out of online play and deletes the EOS anonymous credential and the name tag stored on the device. According to Epic, deleting the App also deletes the device credential. A new ID is created the next time you look for an opponent. This ID is not linked to your name, email address or contact details. Only a number that does not identify you personally remains on Epic’s servers, and it is handled under Epic’s own rules.
  • Purchase history: managed by Apple, Google and Valve respectively. Please use their support or account tools.

11. Age and children

The App repeatedly features fantasy combat between animals. The App is intended for people aged 13 and over, and its App Store age rating is 13+. It is not designed for children under 13, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have received personal information from a child under 13, for example in a support email, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can contact us at the address above. Limiting ad content to general audiences is separate from the game’s own age rating.
